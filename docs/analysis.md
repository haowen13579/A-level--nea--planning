Introduction

For my A-Level Computer Science NEA, I will be developing a Personal Finance and Investment Management System, using C#, HTML, CSS and SQLite.
The purpose of the proposed project is to allow users to deal with their personal finances and investments all in one place. Many people find it difficult to keep track of their income, expenses, savings and investments, especially if they have to use different applications for that.
To resolve this problem I have set up a website where anyone can sign up, log in, and get an overview of his own account balances.
Income and expenditures can be entered, expenses compared, budgets set up, and a fictitious investment portfolio maintained. Besides these features, the application will allow the stock-market trend to be analyzed by calculating moving averages and RSI.
The project will be developed with C# and ASP.NET core for the back-end, with HTML and CSS for the front-end and with SQLite for storing data.


Analysis 
Identification of the Problem

Personal financial management can be a difficult task because there are so many different financial data to be recorded and classified.
For example, the user needs to compute his monthly expenditure, to examine the amount of his savings, and to check the progress of his investments.
Another problem is that beginners often have a hard time understanding the stock exchange data and technical indicators.
I’m working to develop a system that will combine the monetary and investment aspects into one single, all-encompassing web service.

Proposed Solution

I will develop a system for managing personal finances and analyzing historical stock data.
The system will include the following functions:

1 Sign-up and log in. This is where the user creates his own account and subsequently has access to his own information.

2 The application manages income and expenses: Add, modify, delete and view financial data.

3 Budget Management. You can enter your monthly budget and then calculate your available funds.

4 Savings Targets: You can set a saving target and keep track of your savings.

5 Investment Portfolio: Users can record simulated stock purchases and sales and calculate investment returns.

6 Stock analysis. Stocks with the 20-day（ＭA20）, 50-day（MA50） and RSI indicators can be analysed from a historical price chart.

7. Dashboard: Users can view financial summaries and graphs.

Target
My system is aimed at students and young people who are interested in investing and managing their own finances.
Young people who are students and young people who may have no experience in handling money and who may find it difficult to control their daily expenses and their tendency to spend money, may find my method of keeping accounts very useful. It will enable them to enter all their income and expenditure and to make a monthly budget.
Another target group are beginners in the stock market, mainly for people who have no experience in stock market investment. They are difficult to understand stock prices and financial graphs, and my system will provide historical stock market charts, moving averages of 20 and 50 days(MA20,MA50), and relative strength index (RSI) to help users understand the stock trend. In addition, simulation of the stock exchange will also be simulated. Users will also be able to simulate buying and selling stocks without risking real money.
This system is intended for beginners. It should be clear and easy to understand. Its dashboard will allow the user to see his financial position at a glance: his total assets, his monthly income, his monthly expenses, his savings, and his investment performance.
