Analysis

Introduction

My project is a Stock Investment Management System for students and beginners. The system will provide users with a platform to learn about investing in stocks by practicing investing in virtual money.

Users will be able to search for stocks, access stock information and historical stock prices, buy and sell stocks, track their portfolios, and calculate profit or loss.

Target Users

The main target audience for the app is students and novice investors with no prior experience in stock investing.

The system should therefore be simple, clear and easy to use.

Main Problems

Beginners may find professional trading apps difficult to understand.

Users may want to practise investing without using real money.

Calculating portfolio value and profit manually can be difficult.

Users need a simple way to view their stocks and previous transactions.

User Research

I created a survey to find out what students want in the application.

The survey asked about:

Features

Stock information

Portfolio functions

Graphs

Interface design

Ease of use

I will use the survey results to decide the final requirements of the system.
